About

“I often fantasize about simpler, indulgent walks of life – gallerist, bartender, ski instructor – my work is also an externalization of those alternate selves within me, who otherwise would not come to light.”
Biography

Nicholas Shane is a photographer and illustrator based in Manhattan, New York, working exclusively in black & white.

Prints are available in limited editions. Commissions considered for editorial and personal clients.

Inspired by [but not limited to]:
Robert Mapplethorpe, Helmut Newton, Francesca Woodman, Peter Hujar, Daido Moriyama.
